01ABOUT SILVER SPOON

Your neighbourhood table.

Silver Spoon Diner sits on Tattenham Crescent, right in the heart of Tattenham Corner — a short stroll from the station and Epsom Downs.

Regulars have been coming back for over a decade for generous portions, fair prices and a team who remember your order. Pop in for a full English, meet friends for lunch, or grab a coffee to go.
